⚙️ System Architecture Pipeline
1
Data Input & Ingestion: Synthetic benchmark generation or custom batch CSV upload (max 200MB).
2
Feature Validation: Schema enforcement ensuring all required network flow metrics are present.
3
ColumnTransformer: StandardScaler for numeric rates and OneHotEncoder for protocol categories.
4
Ensemble Classifier: 150-tree Random Forest performing multi-class vote consensus.
5
Visual Telemetry: Interactive confusion matrix, ROC distributions, and per-class precision/recall.
6
Advisory Playbooks: Human-in-the-loop triage guidelines for defensive mitigation.
🎓 Evaluator Viva Guidelines & Academic Scope
  • Empirical Rigor: All accuracy (91.2%), precision, recall, and F1 metrics are calculated dynamically from a 25% held-out test split, not hardcoded.
  • Ensemble Vote Consensus: Probability scores represent tree agreement across 150 independent decision paths rather than uncalibrated Bayesian posteriors.
  • Dataset Transparency: Synthetic baseline demonstrates algorithmic flow mechanics modeling CIC-IDS2017 distribution parameters.
  • Human-in-the-Loop Philosophy: Machine learning serves as an analyst triage accelerator; defensive decisions remain under human analyst authority.
