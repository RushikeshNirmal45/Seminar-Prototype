⚠️ Supported Cyber Threat Taxonomy
1. Normal Connection (BENIGN)

Legitimate user traffic adhering to standard RFC transmission boundaries.

2. Denial of Service (DoS)

Volumetric flooding intended to saturate connection state tables.

3. Distributed Denial of Service (DDoS)

Coordinated botnet floods originating across disparate subnets.

4. Port Scan Reconnaissance

Sequential or SYN-stealth scanning to identify listening services.

5. Brute Force Login Attack

High-frequency automated password guessing against SSH or HTTP auth endpoints.

💡 Security Recommendations & Defensive Playbooks
🛡️ Advisory & Decision-Support Scope Notice

Defensive recommendations provide human-in-the-loop decision support and do not autonomously inject firewall rules.

1. Authentication: Enforce Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) and exponential lockout throttling.

2. Volumetric Mitigation: Enable TCP SYN cookies and upstream CDN scrubbing.

3. Recon Defense: Drop unsolicited SYNs silently and isolate internal subnets with VLANs.

4. Incident Response: Follow NIST SP 800-61 lifecycle (Preparation, Detection, Containment, Eradication, Recovery).
